## 🎯 Overview

The Tattoo Stencil Calculator is a preparation tool designed to help artists plan their stencil applications. It calculates necessary dimensions based on design complexity and body placement, ensuring a perfect fit and reducing waste of stencil paper and transfer solution.

**Key Use Cases:**

- Sizing large-scale pieces to anatomy
- Estimating stencil paper requirements
- Optimizing placement for movement and distortion

**Target Audience:**

- Tattoo Artists
- Studio Apprentices

---

## ✨ Features

- ✅ **Area-Based Scaling** - Input design dimensions to see paper requirements.
- ✅ **Anatomy Presets** - Pre-calculated factors for common body areas.
- ✅ **Dynamic Visualization** - Interactive feedback on placement geometry.
- ✅ **Responsive Design** - Works on desktop, tablet, and mobile.
- ✅ **Free & Open Source** - MIT licensed.
