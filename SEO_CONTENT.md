## What Is the Tattoo Stencil Calculator?

The **Tattoo Stencil Calculator** is a free web tool for tattoo artists that calculates the optimal stencil size for any body placement. It accounts for **kinetic distortion**, how designs warp as muscles flex and skin stretches over curved surfaces.

The tool provides four integrated functions:
- **Curvature Compensation Calculator**: Adjusts flat design dimensions based on body part curvature and orientation
- **Placement Guide**: Checks if a design fits a specific body part using circumference data
- **Project Templates**: Pre-built sizing guides for common tattoo projects (sleeves, back pieces, chest panels, etc.)
- **Multi-Session Planner**: Breaks large projects into sessions with timelines and cost estimates

The calculator uses a database of body part curvature factors, circumference averages, and placement notes to provide accurate stencil sizing recommendations. It supports both inches and centimeters, and includes currency conversion for international use.

## How to Use

### Step 1, Enter Design Dimensions
1. Navigate to the **Curvature Calculator** tab (default)
2. Enter your design's **width** and **height** in the input fields
3. Select **inches** or **cm** from the unit dropdown

### Step 2, Select Body Part
1. Click the **Body Part** dropdown
2. Choose from categorized options:
   - **Arms**: Outer Upper Arm, Inner Upper Arm, Outer Forearm, Inner Forearm
   - **Legs**: Outer Thigh, Inner Thigh, Calf, Shin
   - **Torso**: Chest, Ribs/Side, Upper Back, Lower Back

### Step 3, Choose Orientation
1. Select from three orientation options:
   - **Vertical**: Design runs up/down the body
   - **Horizontal**: Design runs around the body
   - **Wraparound**: Full coverage design

### Step 4, Calculate Adjusted Size
1. Click **"Calculate Adjusted Size"**
2. View results showing:
   - **Original Size**: Your flat design dimensions
   - **Adjusted Size**: The stencil size to print
   - **Size Increase**: Percentage increase due to curvature
   - **Print At**: Printer scale percentage

### Step 5, Check Fit with Placement Guide
1. Switch to the **Placement Guide** tab
2. Enter your design width in centimeters
3. Select the target body part
4. Adjust the **Wrap Percentage** slider (25%–100%)
5. Click **"Check Fit & Placement"**
6. Review fit status: success, warning, or error

### Step 6, Use Templates for Common Projects
1. Switch to the **Templates** tab
2. Filter by category: sleeves, back, chest, legs, standalone
3. Click **"View Full Details"** on any template
4. Review dimensions, session plans, and cost estimates

### Step 7, Plan Multi-Session Timeline
1. Switch to the **Multi-Session Planner** tab
2. Select a project template
3. Set **weeks between sessions** (2–12, recommended 4–6)
4. Enter your **hourly rate** (50–500 USD/GBP/EUR)
5. Click **"Generate Session Plan"**
6. Review session breakdown with timeline and costs

## Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

### 1. How does the curvature compensation work?
The calculator uses a curvature factor for each body part (e.g., 1.12x for outer forearm, 1.25x for ribs). It multiplies your flat design dimensions by this factor to determine the stencil size you should print. When applied to the curved body surface, the design contracts to your target size.

### 2. What is the wrap percentage slider?
The wrap percentage slider (25%–100%) estimates how much of the body part's circumference your design will cover. The tool uses this to check if your design fits and to warn about potential distortion at high wrap percentages.

### 3. Can I use this for any body part?
The tool includes 25+ body parts across arms, legs, torso, neck/head, and hands/feet. Each has specific curvature factors, circumference averages, and placement notes.

### 4. What units does the calculator support?
The curvature calculator supports both inches and centimeters. The placement guide and templates use centimeters for body part circumference data.

### 5. How accurate are the curvature factors?
Curvature factors are based on average body measurements and geometric principles. Individual anatomy varies, so use the results as a starting point and adjust based on your client's specific measurements.

### 6. What does "Print At 112%" mean?
This is the percentage you should scale your design in your printer settings. For example, if your design is 10 cm wide and the adjusted stencil size is 11.2 cm, you print at 112% scale.

### 7. How do I use the multi-session planner?
Select a project template, set the weeks between sessions (recommended 4–6), enter your hourly rate, and click "Generate Session Plan." The tool creates a timeline with session focus areas, hours, and cost estimates.

### 8. Can I customize the project templates?
The templates provide pre-built sizing guides. You can use the curvature calculator and placement guide to create custom plans for any design not covered by templates.

### 9. Does the tool support currency conversion?
Yes. The multi-session planner includes USD, EUR, and GBP options. Exchange rates update automatically via the Frankfurter API.

### 10. Is this tool free to use?
Yes, the Tattoo Stencil Calculator is completely free. You can also embed it on your studio's website using the provided iframe code.
